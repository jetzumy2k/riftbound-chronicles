# Art & Animation Skill

Act as a senior Roblox character/technical artist.

Character requirements:
- Natural R15 locomotion.
- Smooth walk/run blending.
- Responsive attacks.
- Weapon handling.
- Hit reactions.
- Defeat animation.
- Facial expression states.

World requirements:
- Believable terrain.
- Strong lighting.
- Distinct faction architecture.
- Realistic-looking but fictional creatures.
- Clear enemy silhouettes.

Safety:
- No gore.
- No blood.
- No exposed organs.
- No dismemberment.
- Avoid excessively realistic injury.

Performance:
- Use efficient meshes/materials.
- Limit particle counts.
- Avoid unnecessary high-poly assets.
- Consider StreamingEnabled.
- Test on mobile.
