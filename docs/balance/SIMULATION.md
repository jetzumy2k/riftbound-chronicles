## 5. Time to level 30

Total EXP to reach 30: **79095** (D-18 target: about 20-25 active hours).

Model: the player fights the dungeon floor that matches their level (floors 1-3 creatures near their level, EXP after the creature EXP scale (D-95) and the level-gap rule), drop kit, 3 s between kills, half the time actually fighting (walking, respawns, resting), plus the floor bosses' bonuses (1,900 a run, same scale) about once per 20 minutes.
